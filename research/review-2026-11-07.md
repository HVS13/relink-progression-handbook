## Confidence and unresolved work

**Strongest confidence:** patch-documented changes, matching node pools and trait tables, named skill ownership, rank budgets, selected equipment legality rules and software totals. Automated checks cannot verify undocumented runtime mechanics.

**Moderate confidence:** the coherence of the recommended skills, character Sigils, rotations and node priorities for their stated Defender roles. These combine source descriptions and specialist advice with editorial judgment; several alternatives can be reasonable.

**Lower confidence:** a universally best skill order, comparative DPS of all 84 styles, optimal ordering at every reduced slot count, and AI performance across bosses/FPS/party combinations. No controlled in-game benchmark across all of those conditions was performed.

Acquisition remains conditional on quest and system unlocks. The separate Warpath evidence records older table provenance and incomplete Conflux conditions; it does not establish that every Warpath must wait until Proud, or that a visible badge trade is usable at Extreme. Paid pack shortcuts remain excluded. Exact joint RNG rarity and a “top 1%” result are not established by legal individual rolls.

Io's raw reaction-summary conflict remains visible in the evidence history. The handbook's correction follows individual duration nodes and the specialist demonstration; this pass did not independently reproduce it in game. Unchanged source text is not new confirmation of either side.

This is a targeted review of the available primary data and identified community sources, not an exhaustive review of every internet guide, video, forum post or Discord discussion. Existing build recommendations are retained because this pass found no newly verified contradiction requiring replacement, not because their global optimality has been proven.
